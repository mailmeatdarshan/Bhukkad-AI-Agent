# Bhukkad Terms, Delivery Policies & Active Coupons

## Delivery Policies & Speed Guarantee
- **Average Delivery Time:** 30 minutes
- **Late Delivery Guarantee:** If your order takes longer than 40 minutes, you get a 100% refund credit in your Bhukkad wallet.
- **Delivery Radius:** Up to 8 km from the nearest cloud kitchen hub.
- **Midnight Delivery:** Open for late-night delivery until 3:00 AM every day.
- **Delivery Fee:** ₹40 (Free on orders above ₹500, or with coupon FREEDEL).

## Dietary & Allergen Standards
- **Pure Veg Segregation:** Dedicated separate preparation areas and cookware for 100% vegetarian dishes.
- **Halal Assurance:** All chicken and meat products are 100% certified Halal and sourced fresh daily.
- **Allergen Labeling:** Nut-free, gluten-free, and dairy-free options clearly labeled on every dish.
