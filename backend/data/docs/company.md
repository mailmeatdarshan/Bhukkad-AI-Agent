# Bhukkad (Company Overview)

**Legal name:** Bhukkad Foods Technologies Inc.  
**Tagline:** Hot, Fresh & Delicious Food Delivered to Your Door in 30 Mins  
**Founded:** 2023  
**HQ:** Bengaluru, India  

## About
Bhukkad is a next-generation AI-powered cloud kitchen and food delivery network. We prepare fresh, gourmet meals across authentic cuisines and deliver them with an average delivery time of under 28 minutes using state-of-the-art temperature-controlled bags.

## Mission
To satisfy your inner bhukkad with chef-crafted food accessible in minutes through voice-first ordering and precision cloud kitchens.
