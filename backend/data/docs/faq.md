# Bhukkad Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Delivery & Timings
- **Q: How fast is delivery?**  
A: Average delivery time is 28–30 minutes across all active delivery zones.
- **Q: Do you deliver late at night?**  
A: Yes! Bhukkad cloud kitchens operate until 3:00 AM every night.
- **Q: What is the delivery fee?**  
A: ₹40, or FREE on orders above ₹500, or with coupon `FREEDEL`.

## Food Quality, Dietary & Refunds
- **Q: What if my food arrives cold or damaged?**  
A: Bhukkad provides a 100% instant refund or immediate free replacement under our Fresh & Hot Guarantee.
- **Q: Are vegetarian meals prepared separately?**  
A: Yes, our kitchens have dedicated pure-veg prep stations and cookware.
- **Q: Is the chicken Halal?**  
A: Yes, 100% certified fresh Halal chicken is used for all non-vegetarian dishes.
